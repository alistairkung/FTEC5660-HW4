Sundial Metering Inc.

Annual Report 2025

Fiscal year ended 31 December 2025

NYSE: SDMT

Headquarters: Tempe, Arizona

Industry: Measurement equipment

Letter to shareholders

Revenue reached about USD 0.88 billion and operating income was approximately USD 95 million. Demand benefited from municipal meter replacement.

Business overview

Sundial Metering sells water meters and the software used to read and manage them.

The business serves customers in the United States and selected international markets.

Risk factors

Municipal procurement cycles and chip availability can affect the timing of meter shipments.

Management’s discussion and analysis

Revenue growth reflected municipal meter replacement. Management uses operating income and cash from operating activities to track performance.

Corporate governance
