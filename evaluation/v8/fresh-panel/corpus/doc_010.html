Alder Quay Logistics Corp.

Annual Report 2025

Fiscal year ended 31 December 2025

NYSE: AQLG

Headquarters: Norfolk, Virginia

Industry: Freight logistics

Letter to shareholders

Revenue reached about USD 1.28 billion and operating income was approximately USD 139 million. Demand benefited from warehouse contract renewals.

Business overview

Alder Quay Logistics operates port drayage routes and contract warehousing for importers.

The business serves customers in the United States and selected international markets.

Risk factors

Port congestion and driver availability can lift subcontracting costs during peak weeks.

Management’s discussion and analysis

Revenue growth reflected warehouse contract renewals. Management uses operating income and cash from operating activities to track performance.
