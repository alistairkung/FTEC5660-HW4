Red Finch Circuits Inc.

Annual Report 2024

Fiscal year ended 31 December 2024

NYSE: RFCI

Headquarters: Hillsboro, Oregon

Industry: Electronic components

Letter to shareholders

Revenue reached about USD 1.03 billion and operating income was approximately USD 122 million. Demand benefited from industrial board shipments.

Business overview

Red Finch Circuits makes multilayer circuit boards for industrial and communications customers.

The business serves customers in the United States and selected international markets.

Risk factors

Customer inventory corrections and slower qualification of new board designs can defer orders.

Management’s discussion and analysis

Revenue growth reflected industrial board shipments. Management uses operating income and cash from operating activities to track performance.

Corporate governance

Board of directors

Claudia Vela, Chief Executive Officer, sits on the board. The independent directors chair the audit and remuneration committees.
