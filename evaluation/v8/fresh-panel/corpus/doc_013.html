Birchridge Cold Storage Corp.

Annual Report 2024

Fiscal year ended 31 December 2024

NYSE: BCST

Headquarters: Duluth, Minnesota

Industry: Temperature-controlled warehousing

Letter to shareholders

Revenue reached about USD 1.05 billion and operating income was approximately USD 124 million. Demand benefited from higher warehouse utilisation.

Business overview

Birchridge Cold Storage operates refrigerated warehouses and blast-freezing facilities for food producers.

The business serves customers in the United States and selected international markets.

Risk factors

Electricity prices and the availability of industrial refrigeration technicians affect operating costs.

Management’s discussion and analysis

Revenue growth reflected higher warehouse utilisation. Management uses operating income and cash from operating activities to track performance.

Corporate governance

Board of directors

Nadia Baines, Chief Executive Officer, sits on the board. The independent directors chair the audit and remuneration committees.
