Sundial Grid Inc.

Annual Report 2025

Fiscal year ended 31 December 2025

NYSE: SDGR

Headquarters: Fort Collins, Colorado

Industry: Electric infrastructure

Letter to shareholders

Revenue reached about USD 1.52 billion and operating income was approximately USD 186 million. Demand benefited from utility replacement orders.

Business overview

Sundial Grid supplies distribution transformers and feeder control systems to regulated utilities.

The business serves customers in the United States and selected international markets.

Risk factors

Transformer delivery schedules depend on domestic steel supply and the timing of utility rate decisions.

Management’s discussion and analysis

Revenue growth reflected utility replacement orders. Management uses operating income and cash from operating activities to track performance.

Corporate governance
